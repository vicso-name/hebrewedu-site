ש

  • שָׁלוֹם

    [shalom]

    Hello / goodbye

    A simple greeting when you meet someone. The same word can also be used when leaving.

  • בֹּקֶר טוֹב

    [boker tov]

    Good morning

    Greet someone in the morning, at a café, at work, or at the start of a conversation.

  • לְהִתְרָאוֹת

    [lehitra'ot]

    Goodbye / see you

    Say this when you are leaving. It is a farewell, not a first greeting.

How to use this phrase guide

  1. 1

    Hebrew with nikud

    The dots and marks around the Hebrew letters are nikud, which help show vowel sounds. Understand Hebrew vowel marks.

  2. 2

    [Transliteration]

    Transliteration is an approximation: sh as in “shoe”, ts as in “cats”, and kh for a sound like the ending of Scottish “loch”. An apostrophe separates vowel sounds.

  3. 3

    English meaning

    Follow the meaning, so you know what you are saying.

  4. 4

    Usage note

    The situation the phrase fits, so you know when to use it.

  5. 5

    Said by / Said to

    “Said by” is the person saying the phrase; “Said to” is the person you are speaking to. These examples address one person.
