PhraseCard · option B “Hebrew panel”

Reference implementation for hebrewedu.com/learn/hebrew-phrases/. Everything here is live: play buttons simulate a 1.8s clip (only one plays at a time), and the Man / Woman switches work by mouse and keyboard (Tab to the switch, then arrow keys). Spec: PHRASECARD_SPEC.md.

States

Hover and focus are forced with helper classes (.is-hover, .is-focus) for display. In production they come from :hover and :focus-visible.

1 · Basic, no audio

שָׁלוֹם

[shalom]

Hello / goodbye

A simple greeting when you meet someone. The same word is used when leaving.

2 · Audio, idle

בֹּקֶר טוֹב

[boker tov]

Good morning

Greet someone in the morning, at a café or at work.

3 · Audio, playing (frozen at 45%)

לְהִתְרָאוֹת

[lehitra'ot]

Goodbye / see you

Say it when leaving. It is a farewell, not a greeting.

4 · Gender variants · Said by

עֶרֶב טוֹב
