# HebrewEdu Semantic Core v1 — Content Audit

Review of all 18 current blog articles using measured candidate demand, existing GSC-export wording, Stage 2G SERP evidence, Stage 2H exact-keyword difficulty where available, and the documented product. This is a planning audit, not a full article rewrite or technical page audit. Volume is Google Ads US average monthly search volume. Difficulty is DataForSEO’s metric, not Google’s. GSC exports are aggregate Web and do not establish US/mobile query-to-URL attribution.

## Audit-wide implications

The alphabet guide, two reading/Nikud pages, and vocabulary method article are the highest-value existing-page improvements because they align with measured demand and/or validated SERP formats. Avoid collapsing the three reading URLs: the exact overlap pilot was low, and intent differs between alphabet instruction, pointed Nikud decoding, and unpointed reading. The app founder stories support trust and product context; they should not replace the homepage for commercial discovery.

Potential new formats (app comparison, printable alphabet chart, common-word list, broad beginner grammar) remain hypotheses. The dataset does not authorize publication; each needs format validation, actual product/content evidence, and a separate decision.
