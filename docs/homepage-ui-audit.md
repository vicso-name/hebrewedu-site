## Executive Summary

- The site is a very small static Astro 4 project: one dependency (`astro`), no UI framework, no Tailwind, no image pipeline, no analytics, no lint or typecheck tooling. Seven `.astro` components, one global stylesheet.
- The homepage is five sections (Hero, Features, HowItWorks, Testimonials, DownloadCTA) between a shared Nav and Footer. Every component carries its own scoped `<style>`; almost none of them use the tokens or utility classes defined in `src/styles/global.css`.
- There are effectively **two design systems in the repo**. `global.css` declares Bricolage Grotesque + DM Sans, an indigo brand, tinted indigo shadows and a `#F8F8FF` page background. The homepage components override this with Manrope (injected globally from inside `Hero.astro`), hardcoded `#f8f8f6` backgrounds, neutral black shadows and an ink-coloured CTA. Blog, privacy and terms pages still render with the `global.css` fonts, so the homepage and the rest of the site use different typefaces.
- The hero contains **no product visual**. The first screenshot appears in the second section, at 213px wide, cropped by a card.
- All five screenshots are Android captures with the device frame, status bar and Android navigation bar **baked into the PNG**. The in-app title visible in one of them is "ALEF-BET TUTOR". None are suitable as large hero-scale visuals (474px wide source for four of them).
- Platform assumptions are pervasive: "Android" or Google Play appears in the title-level meta description, hero pill, hero subtitle, two JSON-LD blocks, FAQ schema, and six hardcoded copies of the Play Store URL across six files. There is no App Store reference on the homepage. (`terms.astro` and `privacy.astro` already mention iOS/App Store.)
- SEO-sensitive items that live in homepage files: the `<h1>`, the title/description props, a three-node JSON-LD graph (`WebSite`, `MobileApplication`, `FAQPage`) in `src/pages/index.astro`, and the anchor IDs `#features` and `#how-it-works`, which Nav and Footer link to from every page.
- Pre-existing defects worth knowing before redesign: `/og-image.png` is referenced but does not exist in `public/`; the favicon is a 118 KB PNG declared as `image/svg+xml`; the homepage `FAQPage` schema has no corresponding visible FAQ; the closed mobile menu remains keyboard-focusable; there are no reduced-motion rules.
- `npm run build` succeeds (19 pages, ~0.9 s). Git state was clean before and after.

## Sources of Visual Rigidity

### Objective implementation observations

1. **Card count.** 12 card-like containers on one page: 3 feature cards, 4 step cards, 4 review cards, 1 CTA card (13 at ≤1024px when the How CTA gains a white card background). Every section after the hero is built from cards.
2. **Repeated geometry.** Step cards and review cards share the same recipe: white fill, 20px radius, `0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,.05)` shadow, ~1.4rem padding, a title row followed by a 14–15px grey paragraph. Feature cards are three identical 380px boxes differing only in background colour.
3. **Repeated "title + small grey paragraph" pattern.** Used 4× in steps, 4× in reviews, and again as "H2 + 15px grey paragraph" in the Features intro, HowItWorks header and CTA.
4. **Uniform section composition.** Features, HowItWorks, Testimonials and CTA are each a two-column split with a heading on one side and supporting content on the other, inside the same 1280px container.
5. **One background for nearly everything.** Five of six page regions use `#f8f8f6`; only Testimonials (`#F1F0F5`) differs, and the CTA card reuses that same tint. No section is white. Section boundaries between Hero → Features → HowItWorks are therefore not visible.
6. **Small product visuals.** The largest screenshot renders at 300px wide; the other four at 213px. In a 1200px content area that is 18–25% of the width. Three of them are cropped to roughly two-thirds height. The hero has none.
7. **Rounded everything.** Radii in use: 8, 12, 20, 24, 32px, full pills and six 50% circles. No element on the page has square corners except the section edges.
8. **Pill/chip count.** Hero pretitle pill, nav-link hover pills, three pill buttons, four step-number chips, three circular icon chips, two circular arrow buttons, one circular arrow link, four circular avatars.
9. **Icons carry no meaning.** Feature-card icons are geometric Unicode characters (`◎ ▣ ◈`) in white circles.
10. **Decorative elements.** Two blurred gradient blobs; a permanent underline pseudo-element on the wordmark; a decorative circular arrow beside the Features H2.
11. **Accent colours.** Amber (H1 accent, stars, avatar), indigo (avatars, blob, hover, card tint), emerald (avatar, blob), lavender card tint, dark navy card. The declared brand colour (`--brand` indigo) is used only in hover states; the primary action colour is `--ink`.
12. **Centred text** is limited to the hero. This is not a significant factor in the current build.
13. **Narrow text measures.** Supporting paragraphs are capped at 320px and 380px and set at 15px, next to H2s of 42–52px.
14. **Typography hierarchy.** Four H2s at three different scales; CTA H2 nearly H1-sized; no H3 level; card titles at 16–18px sit directly under 42–52px H2s with nothing in between.
15. **Vertical rhythm.** Section padding is a flat 6rem for most sections with fixed internal offsets (4rem, 5rem). Hero bottom spacing is 5rem + 3rem margin with nothing visual below the badge.
16. **Hover lift on almost everything**: nav CTA, both badges, feature cards, how CTA, arrow, slider buttons, footer badge.

### Design opinions

- [opinion] The page reads as a sequence of same-sized boxes on one beige field; nothing is allowed to be large. The combination of observations 1, 5 and 6 is the main cause of the "template" feel, more than any individual style.
- [opinion] The HowItWorks staggered grid is the one attempt at an editorial composition, but because the pieces are four identical small cards around a 300px phone, it reads as a diagram rather than a product story. It also degrades the most at tablet and mobile widths.
- [opinion] The testimonial slider hides half of only four reviews behind arrow buttons; with so few items a static layout would present social proof more confidently.
- [opinion] The hero spends the most valuable viewport on text plus a store badge. For an app whose appeal is visual (letters, stories), leading without product UI undersells it.
- [opinion] The feature icons and step-number chips add visual noise without adding information.
- [opinion] Three accent hues in the avatars plus three card tints dilute brand identity; neither the indigo token nor the amber accent reads as "the" HebrewEdu colour.
- [opinion] The warm `#f8f8f6` page colour and the cool lavender `#F1F0F5` panels sit slightly at odds with each other.

## Recommended Future Component Boundaries

Suggested structure only; nothing here has been implemented. All static Astro unless noted.

```
src/
├─ data/
│   ├─ site.ts            store URLs (Play, App Store), app name, canonical site constants
│   └─ testimonials.ts    review records incl. source/store/date fields
├─ styles/
│   ├─ tokens.css         colours (incl. the two page surfaces), type scale, spacing, radii, shadows, motion
│   └─ global.css         reset, base typography, .container, focus + reduced-motion rules
├─ layouts/
│   └─ Layout.astro       + font loading (single place)
└─ components/
    ├─ Nav.astro                 (shared, restyled)
    ├─ Footer.astro              (shared, restyled)
    ├─ ui/
    │   ├─ Section.astro         props: surface ("white" | "tint"), id, width; owns vertical rhythm
    │   ├─ SectionHeading.astro  eyebrow / heading / lede with one consistent H2 scale
    │   ├─ StoreButtons.astro    Play + App Store badges, reads data/site.ts
    │   └─ DeviceScreenshot.astro  wraps astro:assets <Image>/<Picture>; optional CSS device frame; enforces width/height/alt
    └─ home/
        ├─ Hero.astro            H1, lede, StoreButtons, large product visual
        ├─ ProductPillars.astro  short overview of what the app covers (replaces Features; keeps id="features")
        ├─ FeatureStory.astro    reusable alternating text + large screenshot row; props: heading, body, image, reverse
        ├─ LearningJourney.astro the 4-step path (replaces HowItWorks; keeps id="how-it-works")
        ├─ Testimonials.astro    static layout fed by data/testimonials.ts
        ├─ Faq.astro             optional: visible FAQ matching the existing FAQPage schema
        └─ DownloadCTA.astro     closing CTA using StoreButtons
```

Rationale tied to audit findings:

- `Section` + `SectionHeading` remove the per-component re-declaration of padding, background and H2 scale that currently produces three H2 ramps and inconsistent mobile padding, and give a single place to alternate the white / `hsl(240 20% 98%)` surfaces.
- `StoreButtons` + `data/site.ts` collapse seven URL literals and three copies of badge CSS; this is the prerequisite for adding iOS.
- `DeviceScreenshot` is the one place to adopt `astro:assets` (responsive `srcset`, WebP/AVIF, intrinsic dimensions) and to move the device frame out of the bitmap. This requires moving screenshots from `public/` to `src/assets/`.
- `FeatureStory` used several times replaces both the three-card grid and most of the step cards with section-level compositions.
- `Faq` is listed because FAQ structured data already exists without visible content.
- JavaScript: only the mobile menu needs it. Testimonials can be static (or CSS scroll-snap) with four items; no slider script is required. No animation library is needed.
- The existing `@components/*`, `@layouts/*`, `@styles/*` aliases in `tsconfig.json` can be used for imports.
- Placing homepage-only sections under `components/home/` keeps them apart from the shared Nav/Footer, which makes the blast radius of changes obvious.

## Implementation Risks

1. **Shared-file blast radius.** `global.css`, `Layout.astro`, `Nav.astro` and `Footer.astro` are used by 18 non-home pages. Token value changes (colours, fonts, shadows, radii) will restyle blog and legal pages. "Homepage only" requires either scoping new tokens or accepting and checking those pages.
2. **Font coupling to Hero.** Manrope is applied site-wide on `/` only because `Hero.astro` injects a global `:root` override. Replacing Hero without moving that declaration changes every font on the homepage.
3. **Anchor IDs.** `#features` and `#how-it-works` are linked from every page's Nav and Footer.
4. **H1 and heading loss** when Hero and section components are replaced.
5. **Schema drift.** JSON-LD (features list, FAQ, rating, screenshot URL, operating system) is hand-maintained in `index.astro` and partly duplicated in `Layout.astro`. Visible content and schema can diverge without any build error.
6. **Screenshot path referenced in schema** (`/screenshots/screen-alphabet.png`).
7. **Image pipeline change.** Adopting `astro:assets` means moving images out of `public/`, which changes their URLs to hashed `/_astro/` paths.
8. **No safety net.** No typecheck, lint, tests or visual regression tooling. `astro build` is the only verification, and it does not type-check `.astro` files.
9. **Scoped-style specificity.** Component-scoped class selectors override the global `section` rules; new global section spacing will not apply to old components and vice versa during an incremental migration.
10. **Trailing-slash inconsistency** in internal links (`/blog`, `/terms`, `/privacy`) is already present and easy to propagate into new components.
11. **Deploy is automatic on push to `main`.** There is no staging environment in the workflow; work should happen on a branch.
12. **Content/positioning changes are entangled with layout.** Android/alphabet claims are inside the same files that will be restyled; copy decisions should be made before implementation to avoid touching SEO-sensitive text twice.
13. **Node version gap.** CI builds on Node 20; the local machine used for this audit runs Node 26.
